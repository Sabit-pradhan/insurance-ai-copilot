INSURANCE CLAIM GUIDE - DEMO DOCUMENT

Claim Registration

A policyholder should report the insurance claim as soon as possible after
the incident. The claim should include the policy number, incident date,
claim type, and a brief description of the incident.

Documents Required for Claim Submission

The following documents may be required for claim processing:

1. Completed claim form
2. Copy of the insurance policy
3. Identity proof of the policyholder
4. Incident report or supporting evidence
5. Bills, invoices, or repair estimates where applicable
6. Bank account details for settlement

Claim Processing

After receiving the claim, the insurer reviews the submitted documents
and may request additional information if required.

Claim Settlement

After verification and approval, the eligible settlement amount is
processed according to the applicable policy terms.
