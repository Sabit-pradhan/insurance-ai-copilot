INSURANCE POLICY RENEWAL GUIDE - DEMO DOCUMENT

Policy Renewal

A policy renewal allows an existing insurance policy to continue for another policy period, subject to applicable renewal terms.

Renewal Review

Before renewal, the insurer may review information such as:

1. Existing policy details
2. Previous premium amount
3. Claim history
4. Payment history
5. Customer risk information
6. Updated coverage requirements

Premium Changes

The renewal premium may differ from the previous premium.

Possible factors may include:

1. Changes in risk profile
2. Previous claims
3. Coverage changes
4. Pricing revisions
5. Applicable underwriting considerations

Renewal Decision

The policyholder may choose to accept or decline the renewal offer according to the available renewal terms.
