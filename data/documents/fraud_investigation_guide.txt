INSURANCE CLAIM RISK REVIEW GUIDE - DEMO DOCUMENT

Claim Risk Review

The Insurance AI Copilot may generate a fraud-risk probability to support claim investigation prioritization.

A high fraud-risk score does not prove that fraud occurred.

Risk Indicators

Potential investigation signals may include:

1. Unusually large claim amounts
2. Unusual reporting delays
3. Inconsistent claim information
4. Claim characteristics that differ from expected patterns
5. Repeated suspicious activity patterns
6. Risk indicators identified by analytical models

Investigation Process

A flagged claim may be prioritized for additional review.

Possible review activities may include:

1. Reviewing submitted claim documents
2. Checking incident information
3. Reviewing available supporting evidence
4. Comparing claim information with policy information
5. Requesting additional information when necessary

Human Review

Fraud-risk predictions are investigation-support signals only.

They should not be treated as accusations or final fraud determinations.

IMPORTANT:

This is a demo document created only for testing the Insurance AI Copilot RAG pipeline.
