INSURANCE UNDERWRITING GUIDE - DEMO DOCUMENT

Underwriting

Insurance underwriting is the process of evaluating information associated with an insurance application to support a risk decision.

Risk Assessment

The underwriting review may consider information such as:

1. Age
2. Health score
3. Body Mass Index
4. Smoking status
5. Lifestyle information
6. Medical history indicators
7. Occupation risk
8. Credit-related information where applicable

Possible Underwriting Outcomes

For this demo system, underwriting decision-support outcomes may include:

1. Approved
2. Loading
3. Declined

Loading

A loading represents an increase applied to the base premium when additional risk is identified.

The demo calculation can be represented as:

Loaded Premium = Base Premium × (1 + Loading Percentage / 100)

Human Review

Model-generated underwriting predictions should be treated as decision-support information.

A qualified human reviewer should make the final underwriting decision.
